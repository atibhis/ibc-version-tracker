**MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS**


**NOTIFICATION**


New Delhi, the 17th September, 2020

**S.O. 3168 (E).—** In continuation of this Ministry’s notifications S.O. 1049(E) dated 12 [th] March, 2020 and
S.O. 2226(E) dated 06 [th] July, 2020, the term of office of Justice (Retd.) Shri Bansi Lal Bhat, Member (Judicial) as
officiating Chairperson, NCLAT is extended upto 16.10.2020 or until a regular Chairperson is appointed or until
further orders, whichever is the earliest.
