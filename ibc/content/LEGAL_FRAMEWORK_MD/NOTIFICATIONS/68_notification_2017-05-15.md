**MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS**

**NOTIFICATION**


New Delhi, the 15th May, 2017


**S.O. 1570(E).—** In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 1 of the Insolvency and
Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (31 of 2016) (hereinafter referred to as the ‘Code’), the Central Government hereby appoints
the 1 [st] April, 2017 as the date on which the provisions of clause (a) to clause (d) of section 2 of the Code relating to
voluntary liquidation or bankruptcy shall come into force.


[F. N. 30/7/2016-Insolvency Section]


AMARDEEP SINGH BHATIA, Jt. Secy.


**Explanatory Memorandum:** By giving retrospective effect to this notification, it is certified that no one is being
adversely affected and giving retrospective effect to this notification is purely
procedural in nature.
