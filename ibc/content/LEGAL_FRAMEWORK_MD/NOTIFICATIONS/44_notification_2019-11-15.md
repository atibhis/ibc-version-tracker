**MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS**


**NOTIFICATION**


New Delhi, the 15th November, 2019


**S.O. 4126(E).** ―In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 1 of the Insolvency
and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (31 of 2016), the Central Government hereby appoints the 1st day of December,
2019 as the date on which the following provisions of the said Code only in so far as they relate to personal
guarantors to corporate debtors, shall come into force:―


(1) clause (e) of section 2;


(2) section 78 (except with regard to fresh start process) and section 79;


(3) sections 94 to 187 [both inclusive];


(4) clause (g) to clause (i) of sub-section (2) of section 239;


(5) clause (m) to clause (zc) of sub-section (2) of section 239;


(6) clause (zn) to clause (zs) of sub-section (2) of section 240; and


(7) section 249.
