**No. 30/2/2016-Insolvency Section**
**Government of India**
**Ministry of Corporate Affairs**
**5th Floor, A wing**
**Shastri Bhavan, New Delhi**
Dated: 1.10.2016


**Order**


In accordance with the provisions of section 189 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy


Code, 2016, the Central Government, hereby, appoints the following ex-officio members


of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India.


1. Shri Ajay Tyagi, Additional Secretary (Investment), Department of Economic


Affairs, Ministry of Finance, nominee of Ministry of Finance [nominated under


clause (b) of sub-section (1) of section 189 of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code,


2016],


2. Shri Amardeep S. Bhatia, Joint Secretary, Ministry of Corporate Affairs,


nominee of Ministry of Corporate Affairs [nominated under clause (b) of sub­


section (1) of section 189 of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016],


3. Shri G.S. Yadav, Joint Secretary & Legal Adviser, Ministry of Law and Justice,


Department of Legal Affairs, nominee of Ministry of Law & Justice [nominated


under clause (b) of sub-section (1) of section 189 of Insolvency and Bankruptcy


Code, 2016],


4. Shri A. Unnikrishnan, Legal Adviser, Legal Department, Reserve Bank of


India, nominee of Reserve Bank of India [nominated under clause (c) of sub­


section (1) of section 189 of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016].


2. The appointment will be in effect until further orders.
