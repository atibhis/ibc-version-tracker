**NOTIFICATION**



New Delhi, the 18th September, 2017



**Subject: Amendment in the Gazette Notification of Financial Stability and Development Council (FSDC)-**
**regarding**


**F. No. 18/14/2013-FSDC.—** The Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the
notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance, Department of Economic Affairs, F.No. 14/33/2010EM dated 30 [th] December, 2010 as amended vide notifications of even number dated 6 [th] August, 2013 and
5 [th] December, 2013.


1. In the said notification;


i. In para 1, for the word “coodination”, the word “coordination” shall be substituted.


ii. For para 2(b)(iv) to para 2(b) (viii), the following shall be substituted namely—


2(b)(iv) “Secretary, Ministry of Corporate Affairs”


2(b)(v) “Chief Economic Adviser, Ministry of Finance”


2(b)(vi) “Chairman, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)”


2(b)(vii) “Chairman, Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)”


2(b)(viii) “Chairman, Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA)”


2(b)(ix) “Chairman, Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI)”


iii. For para 2(c), the following shall be substituted, namely “Adviser, Ministry of Finance, Department of
Economic Affairs, in-charge-of Financial Stability Division, will be the Secretary of the Council".


iv. In para 5, for the words “High Level Coordination Committee on Financial Markets (HLCCFM)”, the
words “High Level Coordination Committee on Financial and Capital Markets” shall be substituted.




2. The Central Government hereby also declare that Notification No. F.No 18/14/2013-FSDC dated
5 [th] December, 2013 cease to have effect from 28 [th] September, 2015, as the date on which the provisions of Part I
(excluding Section 132) and Part II of Chapter VIII of the Finance Act, 2015 (20 of 2015) came into force.


Dr. C.S. MOHAPATRA, Adviser (FS)
