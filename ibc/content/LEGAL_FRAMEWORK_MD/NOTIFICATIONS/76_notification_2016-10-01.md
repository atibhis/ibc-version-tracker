**NOTIFICATION**

New Delhi, the 1 [st] October, 2016

**S.O. 3110(E).-** In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) and (3) of section 188 of the Insolvency
and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (31 of 2016), the Central Government hereby appoints 01 [st] October, 2016 as the date of
establishment of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India. The head office of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of
