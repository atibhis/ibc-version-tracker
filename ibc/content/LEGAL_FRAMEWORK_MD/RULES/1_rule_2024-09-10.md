**MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS**


**NOTIFICATION**


New Delhi, the 10th September, 2024.


**G.S.R. 560(E).—** In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (zd) of sub-section (2) of section 239 read
with sub-section (5) of section 189 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (31 of 2016), the Central
Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India
(Salary, Allowances and other Terms and Conditions of Service of Chairperson and members) Rules, 2016, namely:—


1. (1) These rules may be called the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Salary, Allowances and other
Terms and Conditions of Service of Chairperson and members) Amendment Rules, 2024.


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.


2. In the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Salary, Allowances and other Terms and Conditions of
Service of Chairperson and members) Rules, 2016, (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 4, —


(i) In sub-rule (1), for the words and figures “Rs.4,50,000/-”, the words and figures
“Rs.5,62,500/-” shall be substituted;


(ii)   In sub-rule (2),


(a) for the words and figures “Rs.4,00,000/-”, the words and figures “Rs.5,00,000/-” shall be substituted;


(b) the following proviso shall be inserted, namely:

Provided that the Chairperson and whole-time member shall be entitled to a salary under this sub-rule
and sub-rule (1) from the 1 [st] day of January, 2024.


3. In rule 12 of the said rules, in sub-rule (4), for the words and figures “Rs.4,50,000/- and Rs.4,00,000/-”, the
words and figures “Rs.5,62,500/- and Rs.5,00,000/-” shall respectively be substituted.



4. In rule 13 of the said rules, in sub-rule (3), for the words and figures “Rs.4,50,000/- and Rs.4,00,000/-”, the
words and figures “Rs.5,62,500/- and Rs.5,00,000/-” shall respectively be substituted.
