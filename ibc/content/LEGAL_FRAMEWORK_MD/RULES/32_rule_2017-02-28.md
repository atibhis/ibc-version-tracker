**MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS**


**NOTIFICATION**


New Delhi, the 28th February, 2017


**G.S.R. 175(E).—** In exercise of the powers conferred under sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 434 of
the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013) read with sub-section (1) of section 239 of the Insolvency and

Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (31 of 2016) (hereinafter referred to as the Code), the Central Government hereby
makes the following rules further to amend the Companies (Transfer of Pending Proceedings) Rules, 2016,



**1.   Short title and Commencement.—** (1) These rules may be called the Companies (Transfer of Pending

Proceedings) Amendment Rules, 2017.


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.


**2.** In the Companies (Transfer of Pending Proceedings) Rules, 2016, in rule 5, in sub-rule (1) in the proviso
for the words “sixty days” the words “six months” shall be substituted.
